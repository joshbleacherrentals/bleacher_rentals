# Preflight — 1.16.0

PR: #407
Status: passed
Signed: 2026-10-07 against 0871cdd

A very big release: 43 commits, 306 files, 14 migrations, a new role (Accountant) and new access
for Maintainers. The database rules are covered by pgTAP (`supabase/tests/accountant_*.test.sql`,
`work_tracker_group_is_paid`, `payment_history_edit_soft_delete`, `maintainer_*`) and every role
has Playwright specs for the new pages and payment screens. What those cannot tell you is whether
**production** is wired up: the migrations, and the PowerSync sync rules in `br_powersync`
(Zones, DriverZones, work trackers, Blocks) which must be deployed with a PowerSync restart.

Merge `7c4026f` had a conflict in `src/app/quotes-bookings/page.tsx` resolved by hand, so the list
page gets a check below.

**This is at the edge of what one person can verify by hand.** The list stays at 14 items by
leaning on the automated coverage; do the production items first, they are the ones nothing else
can catch.

## New in this release

- [x] **Team → open a person → + Add Role → Accountant** (as admin), then sign in as that
      accountant. The sidebar shows Quotes & Bookings, Team, Accountant, Work Trackers, Messages,
      Companies & Contacts and Documentation — no Dashboard. **Accountant** opens on AR with rows,
      each with an Amount Due and Remaining Balance; **AR Deposits** shows the later ones. An
      account manager has no Accountant item.
- [x] **Payments, as accountant, on a booked quote → Billing tab.** **+ Record Payment** a manual
      check, click it, **Edit** its amount (it changes), then **Delete** it with a reason. It leaves
      the balance and the list; **Show deleted** brings it back struck through with the reason
      visible; the quote's **Log** tab has "Payment edited" and "Payment deleted". Open the
      customer's pay link: the deleted payment is not there. Then sign in as an account manager:
      the Billing tab has no **+ Record Payment** button, but the payments still show.
- [x] **Work Trackers → a driver's week, as accountant.** All drivers are listed without
      switching anything on. **Mark Paid** turns green and reads **Mark Unpaid**; click it again
      to undo. Open a trip: it reads fine but cannot be edited and there is no **Release All**.
      As an account manager, no Mark Paid button anywhere. As either, **Mark as Ready for
      Payment** in Payment Details updates the status button behind it without a refresh.
- [x] **Team, as accountant.** The list shows everyone; a driver opens, a non-driver does not.
      Change a driver's vendor and one pay rate, save, reload: both stuck. Name, zones and roles
      are not editable. In **Vendors**, create one and delete it.
- [x] **Messages → Internal, as accountant.** Join an event chat, post, edit your own message;
      there is no way to add or remove anyone. As admin, an event chat's header has **Open
      quote**, and it opens that quote.
- [x] **As maintainer.** **Assets → Bleachers → Add Bleacher** saves, and the bleacher can be
      edited; Documents and Other Assets show no add button. On the **Dashboard**, add a note to
      an empty cell and edit it; open a note written by an admin: it is read-only. No other cell
      action is offered.
- [x] **Quotes & Bookings list, as admin.** Click **Created At**, then **Start**: the order
      changes, flips on a second click, and survives a reload and Back from a quote. Search an
      invoice number with and without `#`: it finds the quote. Search `24600` for a quote that
      totals $24,600.00: found. Collapse the filter sidebar and bring it back with **Filters**.
- [x] **Quote or Invoice.** Open the customer link of an unsigned quote: it says Quote (Devis if
      the contact is French) on the page and in the PDF. Sign it: the same link and PDF now say
      Invoice (Facture), and the admin Contract tab says Invoice #.
- [x] **Quote form → Status → Booked → Save**, on a quote that was not booked. Finance receives
      the "Quote signed" email, once, with no second copy as a CC. Save it again: no new email.
- [x] **Quote form, as account manager.** Pick a contact and company that both have notes. A
      yellow box under Contact shows both; do the same for the Finance Contact. Save: the notes
      appear nowhere on the quote, PDF or customer link.

## Could have broken

- [x] **Everyday account manager work.** As an account manager, create a quote, edit one, and use
      **Send To Client** (the review window opens). **Edit**, **Delete** and **+ Create Quote**
      are all there. This release moved "who can do what" into one function, so a missing button
      here means it is wrong.
- [x] **List filters (the hand-resolved merge).** On **Quotes & Bookings**, use each filter in
      turn — Status, Created, Event dates, Booked dates, Account Manager, Sales Office, In
      GoodShuffle, In QuickBooks — and each narrows the list and shows as a chip. **Show Deleted**
      works, and the search box still says "Search by name, invoice #, manager…".
- [x] **Old payments and balances.** Open a quote paid through Stripe before this release: the
      payments list, the remaining balance and the customer's pay page show the same amounts as
      before the release.
- [x] **Production, right after deploy.** Signed in as the accountant and as the maintainer in
      production, **Work Trackers** and **Team** (accountant) and **Dashboard** (maintainer) list
      real records rather than empty pages. Empty pages mean the PowerSync sync rules were not
      deployed or PowerSync was not restarted.
