## Not testing

- Amount Due maths, SQL against TypeScript — 354 shared cases run in Vitest and in pgTAP, and either side drifting turns CI red.
- Task rules (one open task, creation, completion on payment or Lost, no delete) and who can touch `ArEventTasks` — pgTAP `ar_event_tasks`, `ar_task_sync`, one assertion per role.
- Who may press **+ / –** or move the colour slider — new pgTAP `dashboard_event_panel_writes` (38 assertions, below). The grid itself is PixiJS, which is why the two Dashboard items are by hand.
- Who can reach Collections, the invoice page and the customer's page — `accessConfig` unit tests and the per-role Playwright specs (written, not run).
- Messages tab on the invoice page — the same component as the quote's Messages tab, unchanged.
- Zone/Class lookup for a state written as a code or a name, and the customer-matching and ship-to comparison logic — unit tests on the invoice builder and customer search.
- Nine-digit formatter, padded search, Stripe label — unit tests (`invoiceNumber`, `create-checkout` route). The numbering trigger and backfill are pinned by pgTAP `events_invoice_number`. The real production backfill cannot be rehearsed by hand.
- New Task window position memory and the Previous/Next neighbour ordering — unit tests; only the feel is worth a click, which the New Task item gives.
- The signed-in app frame after the layout change (`SignedInShell`) — unit tested, and every other item here would show it broken at once.
- Specs, design canvas files, `.prettierignore` — not shipped behaviour.
