## New in this release

- [x] As admin, open a booked quote with a Product/Service on its quote type → Billing tab → **Create** invoice. Expected: an invoice appears in QuickBooks (sandbox), the QuickBooks Invoice box ticks itself, and the buttons become **Update**, **View in QuickBooks**, **Check again**. Never **Create** a second time.
- [x] Change the quote's price, click **Update**. Expected: it asks first; after confirming, the QuickBooks invoice shows the new amount and no mismatch warning.
- [x] Edit the invoice's total directly in QuickBooks, click **Check again**. Expected: a red banner on the quote card, a red count on the sidebar's Accountant entry, and the quote listed on Accountant → **Need attention**.
- [x] As accountant, repeat Create/Update on a Canadian (CAD) quote. Expected: it works, tax matches the quote's province.
- [x] /quickbooks as admin: a CAD connection shows the province tax codes and no-tax code and they save; a US connection shows neither.
- [x] /companies and /contacts open as two separate pages; open a contact and edit name, email, phone, notes and language. Expected: every edit is still there after a reload.
- [x] Contact → **Emails** tab (as an account manager with a @bleacherrentals.com login): the contact's Gmail conversations are listed. Send that contact a real email from Gmail. Expected: it shows up within about a minute without pressing anything.
- [x] Open an email. Expected: its own page, thread as an accordion. **Reply**, **Reply all**, **Forward** and **Compose** each send; the message arrives from your own mailbox and appears in the thread as sent.
- [x] Blocked Emails page as admin: add an address. Expected: that person's mail disappears from the Emails tab of any contact; another admin cannot edit or remove your entry.

- [x] Quotes & Bookings and the sidebar load with no errors for account manager and viewer (shared files were edited; the red mark on rows should only appear for quotes with a mismatching invoice).
