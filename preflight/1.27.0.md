## New in this release

- [ ] As admin, open `/repairs`. Expected: **Open** and **Completed** tabs, with a strip of totals in CAD and USD above the table. Repairs that had already ended before the migration are under **Completed**. Open an old repair's row: it shows one service per bleacher, and the old total is unchanged. **If no row ever shows services and the Vendor filter is empty, the sync rules are not deployed. Stop.**
- [ ] Type in the search, tick a **Bleacher** and a **Vendor** in the filters, then sort by Total. Expected: the table narrows and reorders; **Clear All Filters** appears, and pressing it empties those filters but keeps the search and sort. Reload the page: the search, tab and sort are still there.
- [ ] Click a row, then click one of its services, then click the **Reported** day. Expected: the services open under the row; the service window shows the bleacher (number, model, type, zone) and its damage reports with photos; the Reported day opens the damage reports. **Esc** closes only the window on top.
- [ ] As maintainer, press **New repair**: pick a vendor, type an invoice number, set the days, add two services on two bleachers of the same zone with amounts, **Save**. Expected: the row appears with both bleachers and the zone; after a few seconds the Total equals the sum of the services. A second repair with the same vendor and invoice number is refused with a message. A service on a bleacher of another zone is refused.
- [ ] Take a repair whose bleacher has two open damage reports. Change its status from the pill in the row to **Completed**. Expected: a dialog lists both reports; leave one switched off and confirm. The repair moves to the Completed tab. On **Damage Reports**, the chosen one is resolved and the other is still open, with no repair linked. On a driver's phone, only the open one is still listed.
- [ ] In a repair's window, attach a PDF, open it, delete it. Then **Delete** the repair and press **Continue**. Expected: the file opens in a new tab and then disappears; the repair leaves the table, and the page stays clickable (nothing frozen). **Show Deleted → Restore** brings it back.
- [ ] As admin, on the Dashboard: click a repair's bar, then the maintenance icon of an empty cell, then **+ Create → Maintenance Event**. Expected: all three open the same repair window. The cell icon fills in the bleacher and that cell's days. Save one: its bar appears on the grid.
- [ ] On **Damage Reports**, open an open report and press **Create Maintenance to Resolve**, then save the repair. Expected: the New repair window opens with the bleacher filled in; after saving, the report is linked to the repair and the button is gone. The same button on a report card opened from a Dashboard cell also fills in that cell's days.
- [ ] As account manager, open `/repairs` and the Dashboard. Expected: every repair is listed, with no **New repair**, **Edit**, **Delete** or **Show Deleted**; the status is a plain label; files open but cannot be added. On the Dashboard a repair's bar opens read-only, empty cells have no maintenance icon, and **+ Create** offers only Sub-Rental Request. On Damage Reports there is no Create Maintenance to Resolve.
- [ ] As viewer, open `/repairs` and a repair's window. Expected: costs and vendors are visible and the Vendor filter lists vendors; the window has no Files section and no way to change anything.

## Not testing

- Who can write repairs, services, files and categories at the database level — pgTAP `maintenance_schema` asserts every role on every new table and on Vendors.
- List, filter, sort, URL state, totals, status rules, which reports to close, zone rule, duplicate invoice, files draft — Vitest across `src/features/repairs` (utils, db writes, components).
- Repairs page per role — Playwright `repairsAccess.{am,maintainer,viewer}.spec.ts` and the admin specs exist, but parts were written and never run (S43 to S50 for files). The two role items above stand in for them.
- Radix keeping one copy of react-dialog — `radixSingleCopy.test.ts` fails if they split again. The click-through on Continue is still in the list because it is the bug a user felt.
- Backfill edge cases (several bleachers on one repair, repairs that already had services, empty totals) — the first item checks the common case on real data. No live repair had several bleachers when the migration was written, and a one-time migration is not worth a test harness.
- Dashboard grid drawing (bars, stripes, icons) beyond what the Dashboard items click — PixiJS draws cells, so it cannot be automated cheaply and is otherwise untouched.
- Driver app: the new `DamageReportCategories` table and new nullable columns — separate codebase; old builds are covered by everything new being nullable. `asset_value_cents` holds no values yet, so nothing reaches the phones.
- Maintenance budgets, vendor fields and bleacher model numbers — tables and columns only. No screen in this release writes them.
- Specs, design canvas files, the seed script — not shipped behaviour.
