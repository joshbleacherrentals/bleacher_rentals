### 🧾 New role: Accountant

**Team page → open a person → + Add Role → Accountant** (admins only). The role is
for whoever handles the money. An accountant gets:

- **A new Accountant page** (admins get it too). The **AR** tab lists booked
  events with an amount due that should already have been paid; **AR Deposits**
  lists the ones due later. Each shows **Amount Due** and **Remaining Balance**,
  with an info icon explaining both. Same filters, search and sorting as
  Quotes & Bookings.
- **Quotes & Bookings, read-only.** They can open any quote and its Contract,
  Billing, Log and Files tabs. They can tick the **QuickBooks Invoice** flag and
  record, edit and delete payments. They can't create, edit, delete or send a
  quote.
- **Work Trackers.** Every week and every driver, each trip open for reading,
  plus the Payment Details window. They can't create, edit, release or delete
  trips.
- **Team.** They see everyone but open drivers only, where they can change pay
  details, vendor and driver type, and manage vendor companies.
- **Companies & Contacts** (create, edit, delete) and the **internal chat**
  (join, read, post; they can't add or remove people).

Everything else is hidden from them. The **Permissions** page lists it all.

### ⚠️ Account managers can no longer record payments

**Quote → Billing tab.** **+ Record Payment** is now for admins and accountants
only, and account managers no longer see the button. You can still read the
payment history and every balance. If a check or ACH payment needs entering, ask
an admin or the accountant.

### ✏️ Billing: edit or delete a manual payment

**Quote → Billing tab → click a payment.** Admins and accountants can now
**Edit** a manually recorded payment (amount, date, method, payer, reference,
notes, installment) or **Delete** it with a reason. A deleted payment stays on
the record but no longer counts toward the balance, and never reaches the
customer's pay page or PDF. Turn on **Show deleted** above the table to see it,
struck through with a red tag. Stripe payments can't be edited or deleted.
Every edit and deletion is added to the quote's **Log** tab.

### 💵 Work Trackers: Mark Paid / Mark Unpaid

**Work Trackers → driver list, a driver's week, and the Payment Details
window.** Admins and accountants get a **Mark Paid** button for a driver's
week. It turns green and reads **Mark Unpaid** once paid. It's a record-keeping
tick: no money moves, and the week's status and QuickBooks bill aren't touched.
Every week starts Unpaid.

Also fixed: **Mark as Ready for Payment** and **Mark as Draft** now update the
status button on the driver's page straight away. It used to keep the old status
until you refreshed.

### 🔎 Quotes & Bookings list: sort, search and filters

- **Sort by any column.** Click a header. Names go A→Z first, dates and amounts
  go newest or biggest first, and a second click flips it. Empty cells always
  sink to the bottom. Your sort is saved in the link, so Back returns to it.
  The default is still newest created, now shown in a **Created At** column.
- **Search by invoice number**, with or without the #. Typing an amount without
  commas, like 24600, now finds $24,600.00 in Total, Subtotal and Tax.
- **Filters move to a sidebar on the left**, with a count of active filters.
  Collapse it with the arrows at its top, and the **Filters** button in the page
  header brings it back.

### 📄 Quote or Invoice, depending on whether it's signed

**Customer quote page, PDF, quote email and the quote's Contract tab.** The
document now says **Quote** (Devis in French) until the customer signs, and
**Invoice** (Facture) once there's a signature. It used to say Invoice from the
start.

### 📧 Finance is told when a quote is marked Booked by hand

**Quote form → Status → Booked → Save.** When a save is what books a quote,
finance gets the same "Quote signed" email an account manager gets when a
customer signs. It goes out once, on that save, not on later edits.

### 📝 Quote form: see the customer's notes while you write

**Quote form → under Contact and Finance Contact.** Notes saved on the picked
contact and on their company now show in a yellow box below the field, with
**Show more** for long ones. They're for you only and are never added to the
quote, PDF or customer page.

### 💬 Messages: jump from a chat to its quote

**Messages → Internal → an event's chat.** A new **Open quote** button in the
chat header opens that event's quote.

### 👥 Team page

- **+ Add Role** now offers account managers only the roles they can give:
  Account Manager, Driver and Viewer. Developer and Maintainer used to be
  offered, and the save then failed after the person had already been created.
  Administrator, Developer, Maintainer and Accountant are given by an admin.
- The **Deactivated Incomplete Users** window now opens at a fixed size and
  scrolls inside it, instead of growing past the screen.

### 🔧 Maintainers: bleachers and Dashboard notes

- **Assets → Bleachers.** Maintainers can now add a bleacher and edit, delete or
  restore any bleacher. The Documents and Other Assets tabs stay admin-only.
- **Dashboard.** Maintainers now have the Dashboard. They can read events and
  work trackers, add a note to any empty cell, and edit or delete **only the
  notes they wrote**. They can't create or change events, work trackers,
  maintenance or subrentals.

### 🧹 Smaller changes

- **Viewers** no longer see Edit, Delete or **+ Create Quote** on Quotes &
  Bookings. Those buttons never worked for them.
- **Request a Feature** in the Product menu now opens the roadmap backlog.
- **Roadmap.** Quarters are grouped by year, with the current quarter shown as a
  large card. Deleted tasks look deleted, and a sprint's **Show Deleted** lists
  only deleted tasks. Sprints also get a **Hide Completed** toggle.
- **Developers** get a **Dev Tools** section in the sidebar.
