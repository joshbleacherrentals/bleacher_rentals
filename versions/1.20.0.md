### 📥 Mark a website request as Responded

**Quote page → Status button.** A quote that came from the website form and hasn't been sent yet now shows a red **Fresh Inbound** button, with a timer counting how long it has been waiting. Click it to choose **Responded** (you've answered the client but haven't sent a quote yet), **Quote Sent**, **Quote Signed** or **Lost**. Responded turns the button yellow, like New, and you can click it to go back to **Fresh Inbound** at any time. Choosing Responded changes nothing else on the quote.

**Inbound Leads.** Once a request is marked Responded it no longer counts in the red "New Leads!" reminder at the top of every page, nor in the **Inbound Leads** button and list on Quotes & Bookings. They only show requests nobody has answered yet.
