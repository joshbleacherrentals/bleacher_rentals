### 💸 Collections: chasing overdue payments

**Accountant page → Collections tab.** The Accountant page now opens on a new **Collections** tab (accountants and admins). It lists every booking with an overdue payment, with one task each: Name, Next Follow Up, Payment Overdue, Task Notes and Amount Overdue. It opens with the oldest follow-up first; click a column to sort by it. **Show Not Due Yet** adds the tasks dated in the future.

**Tasks look after themselves.** Every overdue booking gets a task ("Remind the customer about payment", due that day), created every morning and as soon as a payment, price or schedule change leaves money overdue. It closes by itself when the booking is paid up or marked Lost.

**Invoice page.** Click a row to open the booking's invoice page:

- **Header:** days overdue, a status (Paid, Schedule, Collections, Cancelled or Not active yet), **Record Payment**, **Open PDF** (the signed contract, or the quote if it isn't signed) and **Customer view**.
- **Information tab:** the task history, contacts, venue, event, what is owed, the payment schedule, the payment history (click a payment to edit or delete a manual one; **Show deleted** brings back removed ones) and the booking's **Files**. You can add files and delete the ones you added.
- **Messages tab:** the booking's internal chat, the same as on the quote.
- **New Task button:** opens a window you can move and resize while you keep working on the page. Pick a date (today or later) and a note, then Save: the current task is finished and the next one starts. It is disabled when the booking has no open task.
- **Previous Task / Next Task** at the bottom step through the list in order. **Back to tasks** returns to the list.
- **Customer view** swaps the tabs for Approved Quote, Signed Contract and Pay Invoice, the page your customer sees, in English. Signing there signs for the customer, and Pay opens the online payment in a new tab.

**The AR tab is gone.** Collections replaces it. **AR Deposits** and **Need attention** are unchanged, and an old bookmark to the AR tab opens Collections.

### 🧭 Dashboard: events are read-only

**Dashboard.** You can no longer create, edit or delete an event here; that stays on **Quotes & Bookings**. Clicking an event opens its details to read, with nothing to save.

- **Colour and bleachers save at once.** The colour slider and the **+ / –** on each bleacher write straight away, with no Update button, and you can take every bleacher off an event. Only people who may edit that event see **+ / –**.
- **Quote button:** opens the event's quote page, for everyone who can open Quotes & Bookings (admins, account managers and viewers).
- **+ Create** replaces **+ New Event** in the toolbar and offers Maintenance Event and Sub-Rental Request. The Create Event icon in an empty cell is gone.
- Moving events between bleachers, and work trackers, maintenance and sub-rentals, work as before.

### 🔢 Every event has an invoice number

**Invoice numbers.** An event now gets its number the moment it is created, including quotes from the website form and events made offline. The events that had none were numbered once, so they can be invoiced in QuickBooks too. Existing numbers did not change.

Numbers now show with nine digits (**000000042**) on the Quotes & Bookings and Accountant lists, the quote page, the quote PDF and email, the Stripe payment label and in search. QuickBooks still gets the plain number, without the zeros.

### 🧾 QuickBooks invoice fixes

**Billing tab → QuickBooks Invoice.**

- **Create** now finds customers entered with only a company name or display name, instead of stopping on "QuickBooks already has a customer named…". If QuickBooks still refuses a new customer because the name is taken, a window offers the one that holds it. An inactive customer is turned on when you use it.
- **Check again** no longer reports a wrong ship-to address after the invoice was saved in QuickBooks itself.
- A venue whose state is written as a code (MT) in one place and as a name (Montana) in another now gets its zone, so the invoice is no longer made without a Class.

The **Permissions** page has been updated to match.
