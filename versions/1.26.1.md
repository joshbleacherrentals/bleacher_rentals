### ✅ Customer quote page: a big button that says what to do next

**The page customers open from the quote link.** Customers kept asking "how do I sign?", so the page now puts one big green button in front of them:

- **Not signed yet:** **Approve Quote** takes them to the signing step.
- **Signed, with a balance left:** the button becomes **Pay Invoice** and takes them to the payment step.
- **Paid in full:** a **Paid in Full** note replaces the button.
- **On a computer** the button sits under the QUOTE / INVOICE label at the top right, and again under the totals. **On a phone** it is pinned to the bottom of the screen so it is always within reach.
- **Right after signing,** the page says the contract is signed and offers a **Continue to Payment** button, so nobody is left wondering what comes next.
- French quotes show the buttons in French.

A quote with **no contract assigned** has nothing to sign, so it shows no Approve Quote button. The buttons also do not appear in the accountant's **Customer view**.

### 🏷️ Clearer step names on the customer page

The three steps at the top of the customer page are now **Review Quote**, **Approve & Sign** and **Pay Invoice**. They used to read "Approved Quote" and "Signed Contract", which sounded like things that had already happened.

- In a quote's **Log** tab, new "Navigated to…" entries use the new names. Older entries keep the old ones.
- The accountant's **Customer view** still shows the old names for now.
