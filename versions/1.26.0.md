### 📅 Collections: see when the event starts

**Accountant page → Collections tab.** A new **Start Date** column sits between Next Follow Up and Payment Overdue, so you can tell at a glance how soon the event is. Click the header to sort earliest or latest first. Events with no start date show N/A and stay at the bottom either way.

- **Previous Task / Next Task** on a collection task follow the same order as the list, so they respect the sort you picked.
- **Export to CSV** includes the new column.

### 📞 Quotes: the sales office's phone on the PDF

**Sales Offices → Add Office / Edit Office → Phone (admins).** A new optional **Phone** field. When it is filled, the number prints under the office's address at the top of the quote PDF: "P:" on an English quote, "T :" on a French one. The online version of the quote already showed it, and offices that already have a phone on file now show it on the PDF too. An office with no phone looks the same as before.

### 🤖 Ask Claude about our data

**Claude → Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector.** Add the connector with the address your administrator gives you and sign in with your usual login. Then ask Claude about our data in plain English, for example "How many bookings were signed last month?".

- **Read-only:** Claude can look at the data but cannot change anything.
- **Who:** every account that is not deactivated, drivers included. Everyone sees the same data: it is not limited to your own bookings. The one exception is the QuickBooks connection, which Claude cannot read.
- **Logged:** every question is recorded, with who asked and what was looked up.
- The Permissions page lists it as **Ask Claude About Our Data**.
