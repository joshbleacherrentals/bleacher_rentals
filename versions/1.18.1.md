### 🚚 Work Trackers: a re-released trip no longer reaches the driver empty

**Work Trackers → a Declined or Abandoned tracker → Back to Draft → fix it →
Released.** When a driver declined or abandoned a trip and you put it back on the
board, the next driver (or the same one) could open it in the driver app and find
no addresses, contacts, line items or inspections on it. Moving a tracker back to
**Draft** or **Released** now fully clears the earlier decline or abandonment, so
the trip arrives complete.

✅ **Trips that are already stuck fix themselves.** Any tracker on the board
that was affected is repaired when this release goes live, including ones a
second driver has already accepted. Their drivers' apps refresh the trip once;
there is nothing for you to do.

Cancelled and completed trackers are left alone, so they still keep the record
that a driver walked away.
