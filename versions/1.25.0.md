### 📄 Export lists to CSV

**Quotes & Bookings → ⋮ menu → Export to CSV.** Downloads the list as a CSV file you can open in Excel or Google Sheets. It has the same columns as the table, and only what you are looking at: your filters, search and sort are applied, and it includes every page, not just the one on screen.

**Accountant page → Export to CSV (top right).** Works on every tab:

- **Collections, AR Deposits, Need attention:** the list as shown, with its filters, search and sort applied.
- **Audit:** the drivers table for the date range you picked. Open a driver and it exports all of that driver's work trackers in the range, not just the page on screen.

- **Who:** anyone who can open the page. The button is greyed out while a list is loading or empty.
- Canadian amounts keep their **C$** mark, so they are not mixed up with US dollars.
