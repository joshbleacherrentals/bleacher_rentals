### 🧾 QuickBooks invoices and contact emails

**Billing tab → QuickBooks Invoice.** Admins and accountants can now **Create** a quote's invoice in QuickBooks straight from the Billing tab. Once it exists, the buttons change to **Update**, **View in QuickBooks** and **Check again**. Update always asks you to confirm first. The app also checks that the invoice total and tax match the quote, and warns you if they don't.

**Accountant page → Need attention tab.** Quotes whose QuickBooks invoice no longer matches the quote are listed here and marked with a banner on the quote card.

**QuickBooks page.** Admins can set the tax code for each Canadian province and a no-tax code (shown for Canadian QuickBooks connections only).

**Contacts page.** Companies and Contacts are now two separate pages. Opening a contact shows an overview card where you can edit the name, email, phone and language, plus a new **Emails** tab.

**Contact → Emails tab.** A contact's Gmail conversations appear here and stay up to date automatically. Click one to open it on its own page, with the thread shown as an accordion. You can **Reply**, **Reply all**, **Forward**, or **Compose** a new email without leaving the app.

**Blocked Emails page.** Add addresses here to keep them from showing up in any contact's Emails tab. Company addresses are blocked automatically.
