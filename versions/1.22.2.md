### 🔢 Invoice numbers now start at 300000001

**Invoice numbers.** Events that had a short invoice number (1 to 9999) and no QuickBooks invoice yet were moved up by 300,000,000, so number 43 is now 300000043. New events are numbered from 300000001 upward. Numbers that were already long, and every event that already has a QuickBooks invoice made by the app, did not change.

### 🧾 QuickBooks invoices: the Class goes where QuickBooks wants it

**Quote → Invoice → Create invoice / Update invoice.** The app now asks QuickBooks where your company keeps its Class and puts it there. A Canadian company keeps the Class on the invoice itself; before, the app put it on the line, so the Class was dropped and the invoice was wrongly flagged as not matching the quote. Those invoices now match.

If your QuickBooks company does not track Classes at all, the invoice is made without one and the page tells you so ("This QuickBooks company does not track Classes").
