### 📍 Proof of delivery by GPS

Every day, each completed work tracker is checked against its bleacher's GPS tracker. If the bleacher stood within 500 m of the drop-off after the driver tapped **Complete**, the delivery is confirmed.

- **Work tracker window:** under the status, a line now says **Delivery confirmed by GPS** (with when and how close), **GPS did not see it at the drop-off** (with where it was seen instead), or that there is no GPS data yet.
- **A driver's week (Work Trackers → a week → a driver):** a new **Proof of Delivery** column next to Pay, e.g. **✓ 120 m** or **✗ 79 km · Main Yard**. Hover it for the full line.
- **Verify → Proof of Delivery (new page):** a row per driver and a column per day, counting each day's trips: **verified**, **not found**, **no GPS**, **waiting** and so on. Trips still not marked complete after their day show as **overdue**, in red, so a forgotten Complete doesn't slip by. **Check now** runs the GPS check right away instead of waiting for the next morning.

### 🧭 Work Trackers: Verify and Auto Generate tabs

**Auto Work Trackers is no longer in the sidebar.** It now lives inside **Work Trackers**, as tabs across the top: **Weeks** (what you know today), **Verify** and **✨ Auto Generate**. Old links still work.

- **Verify:** is the week's plan any good, whether you made it by hand or the model did. It holds the Dashboard, Event Fulfilment, Event Bleacher Requirements, Overbooked Bleachers, Driver Workload, Driver Availability and Proof of Delivery.
- **✨ Auto Generate:** the AI planner. Pick a zone and a week and press the big **Generate work trackers** button. It shows each step as it works, then what it made, with links to review it. The panel holds what the model needs: **Driver Data**, **Event Data**, **Bleacher Data**, **Runs**, **Advanced settings** and **Delete model drafts**.
- Every page in both tabs now has a short line in the panel saying what it answers ("Anyone driving too much?"), and a description at the top of the page.

### 📊 Dashboard & Analytics (Quality Assurance menu, new page)

What the fleet's repairs cost, for a year or for all time, in US dollars (Canadian repairs are converted at the day's exchange rate):

- total spend, the share of bleachers not in an open repair, average spend per bleacher, open repairs by status and how much of the yearly budget is used;
- cost by zone, by bleacher model and by damage category, with a chart;
- the six vendors with the most labor and parts spend.

### 🏢 Vendors (new page in the sidebar, after Companies & Contacts)

- **Every vendor company in one list,** with its type (**Driver** for the companies that employ our drivers, **Repairs** for repair and parts companies), status, services, contact and location. Search, filter by type and status, and show deleted vendors.
- The same vendor window opens from a driver's profile on the **Team** page.
- **New repair / Edit repair windows:** **+ Create vendor** in the Vendor list makes a new vendor without leaving the repair, and selects it.

### 💳 Pay Invoice button on the PDF

- **Quote and invoice PDFs** now have a green **Pay Invoice** button under the total. It opens the customer's page, where they can pay.

### 🔒 Who can do what

- **Verify, Auto Generate and Proof of Delivery:** admins and account managers only. Viewers and accountants keep **Weeks**, including the new Proof of Delivery column. **Check now:** admins and account managers.
- **Dashboard & Analytics:** admins, viewers and maintainers. Account managers don't have it.
- **Vendors:** admins, account managers, maintainers and accountants add, edit and delete. Viewers can only read.
