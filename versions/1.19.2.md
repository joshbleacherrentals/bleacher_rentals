### 🧾 "Invoice" only once a quote is booked

**Quote page, public quote page, PDF and email.** A document used to switch from "Quote" to "Invoice" as soon as the client signed. It now follows the quote's status instead: it says **Invoice** only when the status is **Booked**, and **Quote** for every other status (New, Quote Sent, Unsigned Changes, Lost). This covers the "Invoice #" / "Quote #" label on the **Contract** tab, the title on the PDF and the public quote page, the cheque memo, and the heading in the email sent to the client.
