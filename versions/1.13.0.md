### 📍 Venues: reusable locations with their own history

**Quote form → Venue**, and **Contact form → Default Venue.** An event's
location can now be a named Venue (e.g. "Lincoln High School Stadium")
instead of just a typed-in address. Pick an existing venue, create a new
one, or edit one in place — and picking a contact with a default venue
fills the location in for you automatically, as long as nothing's been
picked yet.

Editing a venue updates it everywhere it's used and warns you how many
other events that touches; deleting one leaves every past event's address
exactly as it was, it just won't be pickable again. Saving a venue at an
address that already exists offers to use the existing one instead of
creating a duplicate.

### 🔍 Contact and Venue fields: search, create, and edit without leaving the field

**Quote form → Contact, Finance Contact, and Venue.** These fields now show
a card for whatever's picked (or "Select..." when nothing is), and clicking
it opens a search box right there — no separate page or full-screen picker.
Typing filters a list of matching contacts or venues, each with its own
edit pencil, and "+ Create New" sits at the bottom of the list. The same
field is now also used for **Work Trackers → Pickup/Dropoff POC**.

### 🕘 Clicking a contact or venue shows its full event history

**Quote → Contract tab.** The Contact and Venue (when set) are now
clickable and open a sidesheet listing every other event tied to that
contact or venue — grouped into Past and Future, each row showing its
status and linking straight to that quote.

### ❌ Marking a quote Lost now requires a reason

**Quote Details** and the **dashboard event modal → Details tab.** Setting
status to Lost now asks why — Out of service area, Sold out, Size doesn't
work, Price too high, or Other (with a note) — and won't save without an
answer. This is what lets us finally report on _why_ quotes are lost
instead of just that they were. Quotes marked Lost before this release show
no reason rather than a guessed one.

The Contract Revenue field also leaves the dashboard Details tab — it's
calculated automatically from line items and tax in the quote flow, so a
hand-typed number there was silently overwriting that calculation.

### 🚫 Declined and abandoned trips now show as a count you can't miss

**Work Trackers sidebar.** A driver declining or abandoning a trip now
shows up as a red count badge in the sidebar, against the week it fell in,
and against that driver inside the week — so work that needs re-covering
doesn't quietly disappear back into the list. The count follows your own
zones, same as the rest of the sidebar.

### 🩹 Fixed: damage indicator strips showing the wrong dates

**Dashboard grid.** The colored strip marking a bleacher's damage window
sometimes started a day late, disappeared for damage reported before the
visible date range, or vanished the moment a repair was scheduled instead
of staying until the repair actually happened. All three are fixed.

### 🏠 Fixed: prepopulated addresses only showing the street

Reopening a saved address — driver home address, company/contact billing
address, a work tracker's pickup/dropoff, a storage location, a sales
office, a maintenance event — used to show just the street until you
clicked in and re-selected it. Every one of these now shows the full
street, city, state, and zip right away.
