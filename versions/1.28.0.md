### 🤖 A new Auto Work Trackers page (early preview)

**Auto Work Trackers (sidebar → Auto Work Trackers).** Pick a zone and a week (Monday to Sunday), and the page checks that week's bookings and can draft the work trackers for it. It is a first version, for admins and account managers to try and tell us where it gets things wrong.

- **Create work trackers** works out which of the zone's bleachers need moving that week and plans them as drivers' days. A driver can do several trips in a day, one bleacher at a time, as long as the day (leaving home to getting back) is 12 hours at most. It only uses days the bleacher is free and the driver has not marked unavailable, and prefers the plan with the least driving.
- **What it creates:** a **draft** work tracker of the **Trip** type for each trip, with the driver, the day, suggested pickup and drop-off times (counted from 7 am), real road distances and drive times from Google, and the **Hauling** line item and pay worked out exactly as the work tracker window does. Nothing is released to drivers.
- **Running it twice does not make duplicates.** A trip that a work tracker already covers is left out. The trips that could not be planned are listed with the reason.
- **Undo a run (admins):** under the button, the page counts the week's work trackers made by the model and those made by hand. **Delete model drafts** removes the model's drafts for that zone and week in one go. A work tracker you have opened and saved counts as yours and is kept, as is anything released.

### ✅ Checks before you create

Down the left of the page, each check shows how it came out for the week (green, or red with how many failed):

- **Dashboard:** the week's grid, grouped by event, with a **Map view** of every driver's route that week.
- **Events:** **Event Fulfilment** (which bleachers are already at each event, and how far the rest have to come), **Event Bleacher Requirements**, and **Event Data** (each event's address is complete, map pin included).
- **Bleachers:** **Overbooked Bleachers** (one bleacher on two overlapping events), and **Bleacher Data** (every pickup and drop-off address that week is complete).
- **Drivers:** **Driver Workload** (each driver's hours, km and trips per day, split into hauling and empty driving, with any day over 12 hours in red), **Driver Availability** (trips on a day the driver marked unavailable), and **Driver Data** (each driver's home address is complete).
- **Fix it from the check:** click a red address on Driver Data, Event Data or Bleacher Data to correct it, with the address already searched and a map showing the pin. The fix applies everywhere that address is used. Account managers can fix addresses only in zones they manage.
- **Leave a driver out:** on Driver Data, **Remove** keeps a driver out of the planning, and **Add back** undoes it. Nothing else about the driver changes.
- **Driver menu:** the **⋮** next to a driver's name opens their schedule, their profile, or their work tracker week.

### 📅 A driver's week as a schedule

- **Work Trackers → a driver's week → Schedule:** a new page showing the driver's seven days hour by hour: the drive to each pickup, the haul, and the drive home. Click a day to see that day's route on the map beside it.

### 🛣️ Work tracker distances

- **Work tracker window:** the distance and drive time worked out for a trip, and the route map preview, now **avoid toll roads** and ignore live traffic. The same trip now gives the same answer whenever it is worked out.

### 🔒 Who can do what

- The **Permissions** page has a new **Auto Work Trackers** row. Admins and account managers can open the page and create draft work trackers. Only admins can bulk delete the model's drafts.
