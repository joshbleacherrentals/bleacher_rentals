### 🙈 Dashboard: subrental rows only appear around the dates they matter

**Dashboard grid.** A bleacher borrowed into another zone through a subrental
adds an extra row to that zone. Those rows used to sit there all year, padding
out the grid on dates where they had nothing to show.

Now a subrental row only appears once you scroll within two weeks of one of its
subrentals, stays for the whole subrental however long it runs, and drops back
out two weeks after it ends. It counts both accepted and pending subrentals, so
a request you're still waiting on is just as visible as a confirmed one.

Nothing about who can see or edit what has changed — the same rows, with the
same access, are simply drawn only when they're relevant. A row you're actively
working on in an open event, maintenance or subrental form always stays put, and
the grid keeps the row under your cursor in place as rows come and go, so
scrolling sideways doesn't jump the list around.
