### 📊 Scorecard counts quotes by the day they were sent

**Scorecard.** **Number of Quotes Sent** and **Value of Quotes Sent** used to count a quote on the day it was created, even if it was sent days later. They now count it on the day it was first sent, and that date never moves afterwards, so changing a quote's status later doesn't shift the numbers. Quotes already sent before this update are counted on the day they were created.

**Quotes & Bookings.** The **See Data** buttons for these two figures now open the list filtered by the new **Quote Sent** date in the Filters panel, so you see exactly the quotes behind the number.
