### 🔧 A new Repairs page

**Repairs page (sidebar → Repairs).** Rebuilt from scratch. Each repair is now one vendor invoice, and the work on it is broken into **services**: one line per bleacher and category, each with its own Labor, Parts, Tax and Freight.

- **Open / Completed tabs**, with a strip above the table adding up the total cost of the tab, in Canadian and US dollars (kept separate, not converted).
- **Search and filters** by Bleacher, Zone, Vendor and Category, plus sorting by any column. **Clear All Filters** appears while a filter is ticked. Filters stay in the page address, so you can bookmark or share a filtered view.
- **Click a row** to open its services underneath. Click a service to see the bleacher and the damage reports it came from, with the driver's notes and photos. Click the **Reported** day to see the damage reports themselves.
- **The button at the end of a row** opens the repair in its own window: vendor and address, invoice number, status, repair days, zone, bleachers, notes, services and files.
- **Old repairs** have each been given one service per bleacher, so none show up empty. Their totals did not change.

### ✏️ Adding and editing repairs (admins and maintainers)

- **New repair** on the Repairs page, or **Edit** in a repair's window: vendor, invoice number, status, currency, repair days and notes. A duplicate invoice number for the same vendor is caught before saving.
- **Services** are added, changed and deleted in the window. The repair's total is added up from its services automatically and can no longer be typed in.
- **Bleachers and zone come from the services.** The first service on a bleacher adds it to the repair; removing its last service takes it off. A bleacher from another zone is refused.
- **Status** can be changed straight from the pill in a row. Moving a repair into **Completed** asks which of its open damage reports to close. The ones you close come off the drivers' phones; the ones you leave off stay open and are unlinked from the repair.
- **Files** are added and deleted in the repair window.
- **Delete** a repair, and bring it back with **Show Deleted → Restore**.

### 📅 Dashboard and Damage Reports

- **Dashboard:** the bar of a repair, **+ Create**, and a cell's maintenance icon now all open the same repair window. The icon fills in the bleacher and the cell's days for you.
- **Damage Reports:** an open report has a new **Create Maintenance to Resolve** button that starts a repair for that bleacher. Opened from a Dashboard cell, it also fills in the days.

### 🔒 Who can do what

- **Account managers can now view repairs but no longer create, edit or delete them**, on the Repairs page or the Dashboard. That work belongs to admins and maintainers. You still see every repair, its services, its costs and its files.
- **Viewers** see repairs and their costs but not their files.
- The **Permissions** page has a new **Repair Costs & Vendors** row and updated **Repairs & Maintenance** and **Damage Reports** rows.
