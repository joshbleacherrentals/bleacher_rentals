### 🛠️ Contact emails on the live site

**Contact → Emails tab.** **Pull from Gmail** now works on the live site. It brings in a contact's past emails from every company mailbox. Admins, account managers and accountants can use it.

**Opening an email.** An email you received now opens with its full text on the live site. Viewers can open them too.

**Permissions page.** There's a new "Pull Emails from Gmail" row that shows who can use the button.
