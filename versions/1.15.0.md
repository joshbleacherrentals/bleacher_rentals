### ✉️ Quotes: review the email before it goes to the customer

**Quote form → Send Quote →**, and **quote page → Send To Client.** Neither
button sends straight away any more. A full-screen window opens first, showing
exactly what the customer will get: who it's from and to (including any
finance contact), the subject, the email itself on desktop and mobile, and the
attachments, each of which you can open. Press **Send** to send it, or
**Cancel** to back out. You'll see a spinner while it sends, then a check mark
once it has actually gone. If it fails, you get the reason and a **Try again**
button instead of a message saying it was sent.

From the quote form, cancelling leaves the quote saved as an unsent draft.

### 💯 Payment schedules are now percentages

**Quote form → payment schedule.** Installments are now set as a percentage of
the quote total instead of a dollar amount. If the total changes later (a
discount, an extra bleacher, tax), the schedule keeps up on its own instead of
falling out of balance with the contract. Every new quote starts with a 50/50
split: half due today, half due seven days before the event.

### 📝 New quotes start with the right contract and sales office

- **Terms & Conditions page.** You can now mark one contract template as the
  default. Every new quote starts with it already selected.
- **Account manager profile → Default Sales Office** (admins set this). A new
  quote starts with that account manager's sales office already selected.

Both only fill in blank fields, so you can still change them on any quote.

### 🏦 Payment details on the quote now match the sales office

**Sales Offices page → add or edit an office → Payment Info.** Each office now
has its own payment instructions, shown on the customer's approved quote. This
replaces the single e-transfer line every quote used to show, which was wrong
for US offices. While you type, a preview of the customer's quote appears next
to the form.

### 👁️ Dashboard: "Hide all Subrentals"

**Dashboard → options menu → Hide all Subrentals.** Turn it on to hide every
subrental row on the grid, whatever dates you're looking at. It's off by
default and saved just for you.

Also fixed: opening an event showed a subrented bleacher twice, once from its
home zone. It now only shows the bleacher's row in the zone you're looking at.

### 🔔 Fewer false alerts

- **Past events and trips no longer have alerts.** Once an event or Work
  Tracker is in the past, its alerts disappear from the alert dropdowns and are
  cleared out overnight.
- **No Transportation** now only shows for events you can still book transport
  for: up to this Sunday on Monday to Wednesday, and up to next Sunday from
  Thursday. Events further out get the alert when that window reaches them.
- **"Double booked" alerts that weren't real are gone.** Taking a bleacher off
  an event used to leave its other events still warning about a conflict with
  it. Those are now rechecked when you save, and the old ones have been
  cleared.
- **Old row-count alerts are gone**, like "15-row: 0 needed, 2 assigned" on
  events without bleacher line items.
- **Bleacher damage no longer adds to an event's alert count on the dashboard.**
  Events were showing an alert with nothing to open. Damage still shows as the
  red mark on the bleacher's cell.

### 🚛 Work Trackers

- **Swapped bleacher.** When a driver uses a different bleacher than the one
  assigned, the Work Tracker modal now shows a red alert saying it needs
  fixing. The reason they gave is shown in full instead of being cut off.
- **Ctrl/Cmd+click on the dashboard** to create a Work Tracker now fills in the
  Pickup and Dropoff Instructions as well as the addresses and contacts.

### 🔧 Maintainers can now work on damage reports and repairs

**Damage Reports and Repairs pages.** Users with the maintainer role can now
open both pages and create, edit, delete and restore records there. The
Permissions page has been updated to match.
