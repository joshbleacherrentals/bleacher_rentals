### 🔎 Accountant page: a new Audit tab for drivers

**Accountant page → Audit tab** (accountants and admins). It lists every driver, including ones who are no longer active, with their distance (km and miles), drive time, trips and pay in the driver's own currency (USD or CAD). Pick a range such as **This month**, or any From and To dates, click a column to sort, and search by name. The total pay sits under the **Pay** header and keeps USD and CAD apart.

Click a driver to see their work trackers for the same range, with a Paid or Not paid label on each, a page at a time. The range and the driver stay in the address, so you can bookmark or share the view.

### 💸 Collections: finishing the invoice page

**Accountant page → Collections tab.**

- **Collections / Deposits / All switch** above the list. **Previous Task**, **Next Task** and **Back to tasks** on the invoice page follow the list you chose.
- **Overdue rule for bookings with no payment schedule.** They are now overdue from the day the event starts. Before, they were overdue 7 days before it. Tasks that this change makes not overdue were closed.
- **Invoice page → Information tab → Task History:** a new **Finished at** column, and the old Date column is now **Due Date**. Tasks closed before this release have no Finished at. A closed task has a trash button; after you confirm, it is hidden from the history.
- **Invoice header:** an **Open Q/I** link, and a **Signed** or **Booked** line under the invoice. Booked shows when there is no signature.
- **Files:** click a file row to open it in a new tab. **Download** now downloads instead of opening the file.
- **Previous Task / Next Task** also appear as buttons beside the page, when the window is wide enough.
- The Accountant page's filter sidebar now starts closed.

### 🧾 Quotes & Bookings: Open AR button

**Quote card header.** Admins and accountants get an **Open AR** button that opens the booking's invoice page in a new tab. Account managers and viewers do not see it.

### 📜 Quote Log tab: payments and files are recorded

**Quote → Log tab.** Recording a payment now adds a **Payment recorded** row with the amount, method and paid date. Uploading or deleting a file adds a **File uploaded** or **File deleted** row with the file name. The Files tab and the invoice page both record them.

### 🐞 Quotes & Bookings: no more frozen page after Mark as Lost

**Quote → Mark as Lost.** After closing the Mark as Lost window, the page sometimes stopped responding to clicks. It now keeps working.
