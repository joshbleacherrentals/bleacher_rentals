### ✉️ Fixed: "Quote signed" emails that never arrived

**Account managers:** when a customer signed one of your quotes, the "quote signed" email to you sometimes never arrived, while the payment emails always did. Thanks to Abby for spotting it.

- **What was wrong:** after a customer signs, the system builds the signed PDF and then sends two emails, one to the customer and one to the quote's account manager. That work was taking about as long as the time allowed, so the last email, yours, was often cut off. It now has plenty of time.
- **The signature itself was never lost.** It was always recorded and the quote was always marked **Booked**. Only the emails could be skipped.
- **Emails that were already missed are not resent.** To see what went out for a quote, open its **Log** tab → **Automatic Emails**.
