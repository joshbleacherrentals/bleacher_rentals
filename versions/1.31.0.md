### 📍 Addresses keep their map position

Most addresses in the app had no map position, and some screens were still saving new ones without it. That position is what the route planner, road times, tax regions and GPS proof of delivery all work from.

- **Work tracker window, "Populate from last / next known bleacher location":** it now copies the whole address, map position included, instead of only its text. Before, a populated tracker was saved without a position, and saving an existing tracker could wipe the one it had.
- **Editing a quote:** saving a quote no longer erases its address' map position.
- **A work tracker made by Auto Generate** uses the event's own address. Changing that tracker's pickup or drop-off now gives the tracker its own copy, so the event, its venue and other events at that venue are never moved by mistake.

### 🗺️ Address Backfill (admins, Dev Tools → Address Backfill)

A one-time cleanup for the thousands of older addresses with no map position, run from your browser at `/dev-tools/address-backfill`.

- **Preview 20** shows our address next to Google's answer and what would happen, and writes nothing.
- **Run** fills them in, 25 at a time, while the tab is open. It copies the position from an identical address when there is one, otherwise asks Google, and only keeps Google's answer when it found that exact building, street number or named place in the address' own state or province. Town centres, road centres and addresses with no province are left alone and listed to fix by hand.
- **Only the map position is written.** Street names, cities and zip codes are never changed.
- **Every run can be undone.** Runs lists each one with **Revert**, which puts the addresses back, except any someone has corrected since.

### 🔒 Who can do what

- **Address Backfill:** admins only.
