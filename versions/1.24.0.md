### 💳 Quotes: every quote needs a payment schedule

**New and Edit quote pages → Payment Schedule.** A quote or booking can no longer be saved without a payment schedule. If it has none, saving shows "Add a payment schedule before saving." The **Remove Schedule** button is gone from the schedule window. To take the full amount at once, use a single installment of 100%.

This way every saved event has installments, so Collections can flag it when a payment is overdue. Events that already have no schedule are left as they are until someone edits them.

### 📅 Quotes & Bookings: change the booked date

**Quote → Contract tab (booked quotes only).** A new **Booked: Nov 12, 2026** button shows the day the quote was booked. Click it, pick a day on the calendar (past days included), and it saves at once. Use it to put the date back when an event moved between statuses and lost it.

- **Who:** admins on any booked quote; account managers only on quotes they created, the same rule as Edit and Delete. Others do not see the button.
- **Where the date shows up:** the Booked column and filter on the Quotes & Bookings list, the weekly booked leaderboard, and the Sales Scorecard.
- The change adds an "Updated: Booked At" line to the quote's **Log** tab.
