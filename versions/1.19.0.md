### 🌐 Website quote requests now arrive as quotes

**Quotes & Bookings.** When someone fills in the quote form on the website, a new quote (status "New") is created for you with their contact, address, dates and seat count. A new **Submission** tab on that quote shows exactly what they typed on the form, since much of it is free text.

- **"Inbound Leads" button** next to the search box: one click shows the website requests still waiting to be sent, oldest first. A red number on the button says how many. Click it again to go back to the full list.
- **Who sees what:** account managers see unassigned requests in their own zones, plus any assigned to them. Admins see requests whose zone can't be worked out or that no account manager covers (Zone 6 today), plus any assigned to them. A request leaves the list once its quote is sent.
- **Top of every page:** a red "3 New Leads!" with a running "Wait: 2d 08h 24m 32s" for the oldest one. Click it to open the list.
- **New filters** in the Filters panel: **Inbound (website form)** and **Zones**, which can pick several zones or "Zone Unknown". A request's zone comes from its state or province.

### 📨 Choose who a quote is sent to

**Send To Client window.** For that one send you can now change the To, Cc and Bcc addresses (suggestions come from Companies & Contacts), the subject, and the attachments: remove the ones there or add files from your computer. The email text and your template stay as they are, and the quote PDF is always attached. Files you add are saved on the quote's **Files** tab only once the email has actually gone out, and the **Log** tab notes each one.

### 🚦 Quote status button

**Quote page → Contract tab.** A coloured Status button now sits beside the contract total. Click it to change the status; it only lists changes that make sense. A quote that has been sent can't go back to "New", and marking one **Lost** asks for the reason. When the contract of a signed quote is edited, its status switches to **Unsigned Changes** by itself, until it is signed again or marked Booked or Lost.

### 🧭 Tidier quote page

**Quote page.** The title now reads "Event name (#1234)" with a line under it: status, contact, venue and zip. The tabs sit centered, and the contract total and the Open in Dashboard, Edit, Delete and Send To Client buttons moved to the top of the **Contract** tab, so they no longer show on the other tabs. The breadcrumb above the title is gone.

**Quotes & Bookings list.** Filters and Show Deleted now live in a small three-dot menu next to **+ Create Quote**.

### ⚡ Quote page stays up to date

**Quote page.** Changes made by a colleague, or in another tab, now appear without refreshing: the quote details, the green "Signed" banner, and the Contact, Event and Venue history panels. Saving a quote without changing anything no longer cancels the client's signature, and line items save more reliably.

### 🛠️ Maintainers

- **Assets page:** maintainers can now save bleachers (the save could fail before) and read, add and edit documents on the **Documents** tab. They can't delete them. Long file names no longer push the Add and Edit Document panels sideways.
- **Roadmap → Backlog:** maintainers can read, submit and edit backlog tickets. The sprint boards stay closed to them.

The **Permissions** page has been updated to match.
