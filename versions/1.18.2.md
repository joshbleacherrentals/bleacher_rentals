### 🔒 Quotes: a price you type is no longer replaced

**Quote → Edit → Line Items → Bleachers.** If you typed your own price on a
bleacher line and later opened the quote and changed the **Event Type**, the
dates or the currency, the app could quietly swap your price for the pricing
matrix price. That is how a quote with two bleachers at $5,400 each ended up
at $1,800 each, and the client signed the lower contract. Each bleacher line now
remembers whether its price was typed by you or came from the pricing matrix.

✅ **A price you typed stays exactly as you typed it** until you reset it
yourself. A line still on the matrix price keeps following the **Event Type**,
dates and currency, as before.

⚠️ **Quotes that are already saved start with every price marked as typed by
you,** because there is no way to tell afterwards which ones were. None of them
will change on their own. If a line should follow the matrix, press its reset
arrow once.

### ✏️ A pencil and a reset arrow replace the lock

**Quote → Edit → Line Items → Bleachers → the icon beside each unit price.** The
old lock icon was hard to read, and a price you had set came back locked the next
time you opened the quote. Now:

- **Pencil** (price follows the matrix): unlocks the price so you can type your
  own.
- **Reset arrow** (price typed by you): puts the matrix price back. It is greyed
  out when the matrix has no price for that event type and those dates.

### ✅ "Prices have changed" check before you save

**Edit Quote → Save, Preview PDF or Send Quote.** When a quote that already
exists is about to be saved with a different price on any line, a **Prices have
changed** window lists each line with its old and new price, and the line items
total before tax. **Go back** keeps the quote as it was; **Save with new prices**
continues. It also appears for a price you changed yourself during the edit, so
you always see what you are about to save. When no price changed, nothing pops
up.
