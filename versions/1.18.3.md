### ⚡ Faster alerts and event chat

**Bell icon (alerts) and Event Chat.** The alert list, the chat list and the unread chat badges were slow to update on large accounts, because the app was doing too much work each time something changed. They now load in smaller steps, so they should feel snappier, especially on older computers and phones.

### 💬 Chat membership changes are all-or-nothing

**Event Chat → Members window.** Adding someone, removing someone, or leaving a chat now happens as a single step. If something goes wrong partway, the chat is no longer left half-updated. Marking a chat as read is also quicker.

### 🧠 More room for your data on your device

The app can now keep more of your data ready in memory, so pages that read a lot of bookings, quotes and chats open faster after the first visit.
